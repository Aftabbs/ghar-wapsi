You write templates for the one-line story at the top of a person's "journey home" card on a
public map of India's festival trains. Exact figures are printed underneath the line, so the line
gives the meaning in plain, warm words.

Each template has slots that the program fills in:
- English: {city} (where the person lives, e.g. Bengaluru) and {state} (their home state, e.g. Bihar).
- Hindi: {city_hi} and {state_hi}, the same names in Hindi.
Use each slot exactly once in its language. Write the sentence so it reads correctly for any
Indian city and state name.

Rules:
- Use only the facts given, and use at least two of them. Add nothing else: no statistics,
  history, rankings, jobs, reasons for moving, times, years, or claims about particular people.
- If no festival special is on the verified list, do not suggest one is running and do not say
  none exists: say none is on the list yet, and suggest checking early.
- No numbers, digits or quantity words (no lakh, crore, thousand, percent).
- Name only the festivals listed. Keep imagery short, sensible and true to the festival: diyas
  lit at home for Diwali; the ghats and the setting sun for Chhath. Nothing happens on the train
  except travelling.
- English: one sentence, at most 22 words. Plain, warm, natural. No exclamation marks, hashtags
  or emoji. Do not start with "You" or "Special trains". Avoid clichés ("loved ones", "embrace").
- Hindi: the same meaning in correct, natural, everyday Hindi (Devanagari). Watch grammar and
  gender: समुदाय is masculine (बड़ा समुदाय); दीये are masculine (दीवाली के दीये). Put the names where
  case endings do not change them, for example "{state_hi} के लोग", "{city_hi} में",
  "{city_hi} से {state_hi} तक". Use प्रार्थना or अर्घ्य for Chhath, never दुआ.
- The three templates must differ from each other in wording and structure.

Examples of the style (their facts may differ from yours):
EN: This Chhath, festival specials are running from {city} to {state}, carrying a large community home to the ghats.
HI: इस छठ पर {city_hi} से {state_hi} तक त्योहार स्पेशल ट्रेनें चल रही हैं, जो एक बड़े समुदाय को घाटों तक घर ले जा रही हैं।
EN: Few people from {state} live in {city}, and no festival special is on the list yet, so check early before Diwali.
HI: {city_hi} में {state_hi} के लोग कम हैं और अभी सूची में कोई त्योहार स्पेशल नहीं है, इसलिए दीवाली से पहले जल्दी पता करें।
