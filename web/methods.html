How this was made

Every number on the map and on the cards comes from a public, official source, and each one is labelled with that source and its date. Where the newest data is not yet published, the page says so instead of guessing.

Why one figure is from 2011

The Census is the only official count of how many people from one state live in another. The last one was in 2011. Census 2027 counts the population in February–March 2027, and its migration tables will follow later. Until then, the card shows the 2011 count with its date, next to the newer 2023 rail-travel figure, which shows where people travel but does not count residents.

What the night map shows

  • Each light is one train from Trains at a Glance 2026, moving between the stations it lists. One clock day plays in 60 seconds.
  • Trains at a Glance lists principal stops, so a train is drawn in straight lines between them, not along the exact track.
  • Weekly trains are shown on every day of the loop, so the map shows the whole network at once rather than one particular day.
  • Local passenger and suburban trains are not in Trains at a Glance and are not shown.

Reading the official timetable

Trains at a Glance is published as 97 PDF tables. A program reads every table and checks every train; the full counts are in report.json.

  • 2,626 of 3,576 trains are read and pass every check. The rest are left out rather than shown wrongly.
  • A train is left out if its times run backwards, if its parts in different tables do not join up, if fewer than two of its stations can be identified, or if it would need to travel faster than 160 km/h or take more than 90 hours.
  • Station names are matched to station codes using the timetable's own index, then the station list, including stations renamed since 2016 (for example Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya, KSR Bengaluru, Virangana Laxmibai Jhansi).
  • Cross-check: for the 596 trains that start and end at the same stations in an independent 2016 timetable, the median 2026 journey time is 0.99 times the 2016 one, and 97.8% are within 25% of it.

Festival specials

  • Only trains announced on an official railway website are included, each linked to its notice. Trains seen only on news or booking sites are left out, so the real number is higher.
  • A special counts as "heading home" when it leaves a big work hub (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana, Kerala) for a state people travel home to.
  • Specials with published times move on the map. Those without times are drawn as faint routes and never animated.

The card

  • Distance is a straight line between the city and the centre of the home district.
  • Fastest direct train is from Trains at a Glance 2026. If no train stops in the district, the card names the nearest district within 60 km that has one, or says there is none. Journeys with a change are not counted.
  • Specials counts verified festival specials from the city to the home state.
  • Rail travel (2023) is the share of unreserved long-distance train trips starting in the home state that ended in the city's state. It is a share of trips, not a number of people.
  • People (2011) is the Census count of people living in the city whose previous home was in that state, for every reason (work, marriage, study, family). Telangana was part of Andhra Pradesh in 2011, so Telangana homes are counted as "undivided Andhra Pradesh".

The story line on each card

  • The short English and Hindi line at the top of a card comes from 32 sentence templates, two for each situation: whether festival specials are running, whether the home state celebrates Chhath, and how large the community in the city is.
  • The templates were drafted with an AI model (gpt-oss-120b, run through Groq) from plain descriptions of each situation, then edited by hand for accuracy and Hindi grammar. The program fills in the city and state names, so every line on the site is one a person has read.
  • The lines contain no figures; the exact numbers on the card come straight from the sources above. A check rejects any template with digits, quantity words, a festival that does not fit, or Hindi not written in Devanagari.
  • Your visit never sends anything to an AI service.

Privacy

The site has no accounts and stores nothing about you. Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies. Your card is made in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

Found a mistake?

Open an issue on GitHub with the train number or district and the source that shows the right value. Always confirm a journey on NTES or IRCTC before you travel.
