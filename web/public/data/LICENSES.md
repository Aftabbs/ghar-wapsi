# Data sources and terms

| File | Contents | Source and terms |
|---|---|---|
| `trains-long.bin`, `trains-rest.bin`, `trains.meta.json`, `explore.json`, `fares.json` | Train schedules derived from *Trains at a Glance 2026* | Ministry of Railways (Railway Board). Timings are reproduced as published, with the source acknowledged, under the Indian Railways portal's reproduction terms. |
| `specials-2026.json` | Special trains by season (now Diwali and Chhath 2026) | Zonal railway press releases; each row links to its notice. |
| `rail-travel-2023.json` | Shares of 2023 rail trips between states | EAC-PM, *400 Million Dreams* (Debroy & Misra, 2024), Table 7. Cited, not altered. |
| `migration-2011.json` | Migrants by last residence | Census of India 2011, Table D-02 (Office of the Registrar General). |
| `stations.odbl.json` | Station codes and locations | DataMeet (CC0) merged with OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). |
| `places.json` | Districts under today's names | Census 2011 district list and boundaries, with today's names and states added by hand. |
| `towns/*.json` | Towns, villages and small stations | [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) (CC BY 4.0), with station names from `stations.odbl.json` above. |
| `india-states.json` | State boundaries | DataMeet maps (MIT), following the Survey of India outline. |
