# Data sources and terms

| File | Contents | Source and terms |
|---|---|---|
| `trains-long.bin`, `trains-rest.bin`, `trains.meta.json`, `routes.json` | Train schedules derived from *Trains at a Glance 2026* | Ministry of Railways (Railway Board). Timings are reproduced as published, with the source acknowledged, under the Indian Railways portal's reproduction terms. |
| `specials-2026.json` | 2026 festival special trains | Zonal railway press releases; each row links to its notice. |
| `rail-travel-2023.json` | Shares of 2023 rail trips between states | EAC-PM, *400 Million Dreams* (Debroy & Misra, 2024), Table 7. Cited, not altered. |
| `migration-2011.json` | Migrants by last residence | Census of India 2011, Table D-02 (Office of the Registrar General). |
| `stations.odbl.json` | Station codes and locations | DataMeet (CC0) merged with OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). |
| `india-states.json` | State boundaries | DataMeet maps (MIT), following the Survey of India outline. |
